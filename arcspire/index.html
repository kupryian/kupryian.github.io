English — About Arcspire

One tap. One more climb.

Arcspire is an offline arcade game for Android. Tap at the right moment to launch an orb along a tangent and land on the next orbit. Learn the rhythm, find your trajectory and keep climbing.

  • 5,000 campaign levels and additional Master Sector challenges.
  • Endless mode for personal records and Daily challenges.
  • Modules, cosmetic customization and a constellation of completed levels.
  • Local progress saving and accessibility settings.

The current version has no accounts, advertisements or real-money purchases. Arcspire is in closed testing; installation depends on access to the test.
